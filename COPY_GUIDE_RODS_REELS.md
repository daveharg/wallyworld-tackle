# Rod & Reel Copy Rewrite — Style Guide (2026-10-06)

## Dave's standing rules (must follow)
- Tagline: "good gear, low prices."
- NEVER call products "cheap". Use Budget / Value / Mid-Range / Premium.
- Say "chosen", never "tested".
- Carbon fibre rods MUST say so in the product title.
- No Bass Pro (or any brand) comparisons.
- Never invent specs, features, prices, or claims. Every feature must come from: the product's spec text, its images (spec charts/callouts), or its variant options.
- Honest marketing.

## Titles
Format: `{Brand} {Series/Model} {Carbon Fiber if carbon} {Spinning/Casting/Telescopic/Ice} {Rod|Reel} — {1-2 standout specs}`
- Kill keyword stuffing. No "Pesca", no repeated phrases, no "Fishing Rod Fishing Pole" duplication.
- Keep under ~110 characters.
- Carbon rule: if the blank is carbon (any carbon content per specs), the title MUST include "Carbon" or "Carbon Fiber".
- Lead with what matters: brand + series + type + key spec (length range, gear ratio, max drag).
- Examples:
  - BAD: "Sougayilang 2 Sections Casting/Spinning Fishing Rod 1.8-2.1m Carbon Fiber Portable Travel Rod for All Water Fishing Pole Pesca"
  - GOOD: "Sougayilang Carbon Spinning/Casting Rod — 1.8–2.1m, 2-Piece Travel"
  - GOOD: "BILLINGS SD Baitcasting Reel — 6.3:1, 35 lb Max Drag, Carbon Shell"

## Descriptions
Structure:
1. Hook (1–2 sentences): what it is and who it's for. Attractive, honest. No hype words like "revolutionary".
2. Key features (bullets): EVERY real feature from specs/images — carbon tonnage (24T/30T/36T), guide type (ceramic/Fuji), handle (EVA/cork), sections, gear ratio, bearings (5+1BB etc.), max drag, spool material, brake system, anti-reverse, line capacity. If it's printed on a product image, it goes here.
3. Specs: variant-level facts (lengths, line weight, lure weight per option).
4. Good for: species/techniques ONLY as far as specs support (e.g. 1–6 lb ultralight → trout, perch, panfish; 8–17 lb medium → bass, walleye, pike). Conservative, no promises.

## Tone
Knowledgeable tackle-shop owner. Plain words, short sentences. Enthusiastic but straight.
